Can I download videos from YouTube and Facebook with Grabby?

In short: YouTube's and Meta's rules don't allow it, but breaking those rules is not a crime in itself. What really matters under the law is which video you download and what you do with it afterwards. For private use, on your own computer, the practical risk is low; it becomes serious if you share the videos or distribute the app to others.

This is not legal advice. It is informational research based on public sources (listed at the bottom), with reference to Italian and EU law. For important decisions, such as publishing the app or using it for work, ask a lawyer who specialises in copyright.

The answer in three lines
Does YouTube allow it? No

The Terms forbid downloading content except through official features (e.g. YouTube Premium offline) or with written permission.

Does Meta allow it? No

The Terms forbid accessing or collecting data by automated means without permission, and infringing other people's copyright.

Is it illegal in Italy? It depends

Your own videos or freely licensed ones: no problem. Other people's videos: a grey area for private use, unlawful if you redistribute them.

The traffic light: case by case

Using videos and audio downloaded with Grabby is allowed only in the green cases.

Videos you uploaded yourselfThey are yours. YouTube Studio and Facebook's "Download your information" feature already offer them; using Grabby instead technically breaches the Terms, but nobody else's rights are involved.
Green

Yellow

What YouTube's Terms say

In the "Permissions and Restrictions" section of its Terms of Service, YouTube lists what you may not do. Three points directly concern Grabby:

"access, reproduce, download, distribute […] any part of the Service or any Content", except where expressly authorised by the Service or with prior written permission from YouTube and the rights holders.YouTube Terms, restriction 1
"circumvent, disable, fraudulently engage with, or otherwise interfere with any part of the Service"YouTube Terms, restriction 2
"access the Service using any automated means (such as robots, botnets or scrapers)", except public search engines or with written permission.YouTube Terms, restriction 3

The only "official" way to watch a video offline is the download feature in the YouTube app (with Premium), which keeps the file inside the app and does not save it to your computer.

What you risk in practice

  • The Terms are a contract: breaching them is not a crime. The typical consequence is that YouTube blocks access (errors, "confirm you're not a bot" checks, temporary IP blocks).
  • If you use your Google account's cookies to download, YouTube could in theory suspend the account. Grabby does not use them.
  • YouTube often changes its mechanisms to hinder external tools: that is a technical reason to keep yt-dlp up to date, not a legal risk in itself.

What Meta's (Facebook's) Terms say

Meta's Terms of Service (version in force since 1 January 2025, section "What you can share and do") contain two relevant prohibitions:

"You may not access or collect data from our Products using automated means (without our prior permission)", even when you are not logged in to an account.Meta Terms, sec. 3.2
You may not use Meta Products to do anything that "infringes or violates someone else's rights, including their intellectual property rights".Meta Terms, sec. 3.2

Facebook offers no button to download other people's videos. For your own content there is the "Download your information" tool. Many Facebook videos contain music licensed only for use inside the platform, so an external copy may also affect music rights.

What Italian and EU law say

The Italian copyright law (L. 633/1941) is what really matters, because it applies regardless of the platforms' Terms.

ProvisionWhat it says, in plain wordsWhy it concerns you
Art. 71-sexies(1)You may make a private copy of audio and video, for strictly personal use, not for profit, respecting technical protection measures.This is what makes a copy for yourself possible.
Art. 71-sexies(2)A private copy cannot be made by third parties on your behalf. A service that makes copies for users is treated as unauthorised reproduction.A local app is fine; a "Grabby online" website for others is not.
Art. 71-sexies(3)The private copy does not apply to works made available online "on demand" if protected by technical measures or governed by a contract.This is exactly the case of YouTube and Facebook: this is where the grey area comes from.
Art. 102-quaterRights holders may protect works with technological measures; circumventing them is forbidden.yt-dlp does not touch DRM-protected videos. The question is whether YouTube's protections count as an "effective measure".
Art. 174-terAnyone who unlawfully duplicates or reproduces protected works, even for themselves, risks an administrative fine (€154, plus confiscation and publication of the decision).This is the typical risk for personal use not covered by the private copy.
Art. 171Making protected works available to the public online without the right to do so is a criminal offence.It applies if you re-upload or share the downloaded videos.
Art. 171-terFor non-personal uses it punishes, among others, those who distribute tools whose main purpose is to circumvent technological measures.Relevant only if you ever distributed the app to others.
CJEU C-435/12 (ACI Adam, 2014)A private copy may only be made from a lawful source.A pirated film uploaded to YouTube may not be downloaded, not even for yourself.

Known cases about youtube-dl and yt-dlp

Grabby uses yt-dlp, which derives from youtube-dl. These precedents show where the risk lies.

October 2020 · United States

The RIAA gets youtube-dl removed from GitHub

The American recording industry association called it a tool for circumventing protections (DMCA, section 1201), citing some music videos used in the code's tests.

November 2020 · United States

GitHub reinstates it

After a letter from the EFF, GitHub concluded that youtube-dl does not circumvent any protection and acknowledged its legitimate uses (journalism, accessibility, freely licensed videos).

March 2023 · Germany

The Hamburg court rules against the site's host

Sony, Warner and Universal sued Uberspace, which hosted the youtube-dl website. The court considered YouTube's mechanism an effective technical measure.

November 2024 · Germany

The Hamburg Court of Appeal confirms

The case concerns those who distribute the tool, not those who use it. youtube-dl and yt-dlp remain available on GitHub.
